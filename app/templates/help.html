Importing books on an old iPad

EPUB books

Tap the Open in iBooks button on a book. Safari will offer to open it in iBooks or Books. Choose it and the book is added to your library.

PDF books

Tap Open PDF. Safari shows the PDF. Tap the page once if the buttons disappear, then tap the Share button (the box with an arrow) and choose Copy to iBooks, Add to iBooks, or Open in Books.

If nothing happens

Press and hold the button, then choose Open or Open in New Tab.

If the file name looks wrong

If a book downloads as .zip or .epub.zip, the server headers need adjusting — let the administrator know.

Trouble connecting

Make sure the iPad is on the same Wi-Fi network as the server, and that you typed the address exactly.
